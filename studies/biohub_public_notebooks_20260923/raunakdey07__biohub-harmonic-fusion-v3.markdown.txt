## Pipeline Architecture & Notebook Contents

This notebook represents a highly tuned, robust inference pipeline utilizing dual temporal models, bidirectional association matching, and strict biological geometry validation.

### 1. Dual Temporal Models & Edge-Feature TTA
- **Ensemble Blending:** Utilizes two independent temporal UNet models (Primary and Secondary Seed 314159). The secondary detection weight has been aggressively boosted to `0.80` to heavily leverage the blended consensus.
- **Dual Edge-Feature TTA:** Introduces a massive upgrade to the inference engine. Both the primary and secondary models now utilize an exhaustive 8-way spatial Test-Time Augmentation (D4 flips, rotations, and transposes) strictly on the extracted edge features, drastically stabilizing connection probabilities.

### 2. Bidirectional Harmonic Association Fusion
- The Node-Transformer predicts edges in both **forward** (t → t+1) and **reverse** (t+1 → t) time directions.
- A weighted harmonic mean fuses the probabilities (weighted 0.15 toward the reverse direction). Because the harmonic mean heavily penalizes large discrepancies, spurious one-directional confident links are eradicated before they ever reach the ILP solver.

### 3. "Wide" Geometry-Validated Divisions
- The safe-division recovery constraints have been completely overhauled to cast a wider but stricter net.
- **Expanded Search Radius:** Parent max distance expanded to `9.0 µm` and sister max distance expanded to `14.0 µm`.
- **Strict Geometric Validation:** To counteract the wider search radius, divisions are gated by strict new biological checks:
  - **Sister Symmetry Tau (`0.6`):** Prevents highly asymmetric daughter cell trajectories.
  - **Divergence (`2.25 µm`):** Requires daughter cells to physically diverge from one another in subsequent frames to validate a true division.
  - **Mutual Nearest Neighbors:** Enforces reciprocal spatial proximity between candidate daughter cells.

### 4. Adaptive Density Gap Closing & Gap2 Recovery
- **2-Frame Gap Recovery:** `GAP2_RECOVERY` is activated, allowing the pipeline to successfully stitch trajectories across longer occlusions.
- **KDTree Adaptive Density:** Gap closing dynamically scales its connection threshold based on local cell crowding, utilizing a spatial `cKDTree` to measure the median spacing of the 3 nearest neighbors, preventing aggressive gap-closes in dense clusters.

### 5. DeepCenter Veto Gate
- An independent `_DCDeepCenterUNet3D` spatial heatmap acts as an absolute gatekeeper. It evaluates the spatial coordinates of synthetic gap-closure nodes and safe-division candidates, vetoing any proposed tracks that land in low-probability background regions.
